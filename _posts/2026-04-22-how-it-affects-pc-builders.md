# How the RAM Shortage Is Hitting PC Builders

*April 22, 2026*

For DIY PC enthusiasts, the RAM shortage has made one of the cheapest upgrade paths suddenly painful.

## Price Trends

A standard 32GB DDR5-6000 kit that cost around $80 in mid-2024 now retails for $130–$160. 64GB workstation kits have nearly doubled in some regions. DDR4 has also crept up as manufacturers wind down production in favor of DDR5.

## What You Should Do

**If you need RAM urgently:** Buy now. Prices are unlikely to drop in the near term, and waiting risks paying even more in six months.

**If you can wait:** Target Q3 or Q4 2027, when new fab supply should start easing prices.

**Consider used:** The used DDR4 market is healthy. Verify specs and test with MemTest86 before trusting any used kit.

## What to Avoid

- Overpriced RGB "gaming" kits — the underlying chips are identical to plain ones
- No-name brands with no warranty
- Buying more than you currently need
