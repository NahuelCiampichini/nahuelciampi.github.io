# AI Is Eating the World's RAM

*May 1, 2026*

The single biggest driver of the current RAM shortage isn't smartphones or gaming PCs — it's artificial intelligence.

## The Core Problem: HBM Is the Chokepoint

High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM) — the stacked DRAM used in AI accelerators — is manufactured on the same equipment as conventional DRAM. When SK Hynix dedicates a fab line to HBM3E for Nvidia, those wafers can't produce consumer DDR5.

SK Hynix, which supplies the majority of HBM to Nvidia, has been running at full HBM capacity for over a year. Samsung is racing to qualify its own HBM3E. Micron is a distant third.

## The Trickle-Down Effect

1. HBM demand pulls fab capacity away from standard DRAM
2. Standard DRAM supply tightens
3. Enterprise RDIMM gets prioritized over consumer UDIMM
4. Consumer prices rise

It's not that AI companies are buying your DDR5 kit — it's that they're consuming the manufacturing capacity that would have produced it.

## Will It Get Better?

New HBM-specific fab lines are being built that won't compete with conventional DRAM. But those take 2–3 years to come online, and the AI infrastructure buildout shows no signs of slowing.
