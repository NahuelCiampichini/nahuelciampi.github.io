Welcome to my new Jekyll-powered website! I'm excited to share this space with you.

## Why Jekyll?

Jekyll is a fantastic static site generator that makes it easy to:

- **Write in Markdown** — No complex CMS needed
- **Version control everything** — Your whole site lives in Git
- **Deploy for free** — GitHub Pages hosts Jekyll sites at no cost
- **Stay fast** — Static HTML loads lightning fast

## The Midnight Theme

I chose the [Midnight theme](https://github.com/pages-themes/midnight) for its clean, dark aesthetic that's easy on the eyes and lets content shine.
